Privacy

{app} shares things out fairly in a group: someone creates a lottery, shares its link, and each person ranks what they want. There are no accounts and no emails. This page says what {app} keeps, who sees it and what you can ask for.

What we keep

  • Each lottery: its name, what it shares out and their names, the names people join with, each person's ranking and what the lottery gave them.
  • Its record: what the lottery read and decided, kept as it was written, so that anyone can check the result.

Nobody gives an email, a password or anything else about themselves. A name is whatever someone types: it can be a nickname. Your internet address (IP) is used for a short while to limit how often anyone can create, join or rank; it is not kept with the lottery.

Who sees it

  • Anyone with the link sees who joined and who has ranked, but not what they ranked.
  • Once the lottery runs, anyone with the link sees each person's ranking and what they got, since checking the result needs them.
  • Lotteries are never listed, and search engines are asked not to show them.
  • The {app} team can see what is needed to keep the site working.

We do not sell anything, show ads or share it with anyone else.

Cookies and your device

Your browser keeps a secret for each lottery you join or create, so that it knows you when you come back. Only a code made from that secret is stored here: nobody can work the secret out from it. Other cookies protect your forms and remember your language. There are no advertising or tracking cookies.

Your device keeps a copy of the pages you visited, so that they open without a connection.

How long

A lottery nobody has touched for 90 days is deleted, with its people and its record. Each lottery's page says when. When something goes wrong, we note where, to fix it, without saying who was using it.

Your rights

You can leave a lottery until it runs, and its organizer can remove anyone; the name stays in its record, marked as gone, until the lottery is deleted. To have a lottery deleted sooner, or to ask anything about your data, write to {contact} with the lottery's link. We answer within the terms of Uruguay's personal data protection law (Ley N.º 18.331); you can also turn to its authority, the Unidad Reguladora y de Control de Datos Personales (URCDP).

Changes

If this page changes in anything that matters, we will say so on the site before it applies.
