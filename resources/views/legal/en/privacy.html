Privacy

{app} is where housing cooperatives organize their projects, up to the lottery that assigns each family its home. This page says what {app} keeps about you, who sees it and what you can ask for. It applies to this site, run by the {app} team.

What we keep

  • Your account: your name, email and password (stored scrambled: nobody can read it). If you add them: your phone, a few words about you, your picture and your ID number.
  • What you do in your projects: your family, your family's ranking of the units, your answers to events, the files you share, your posts in the forum and what the lottery assigned.
  • Your preferences: your language and how the app looks.

If you sign in with Google, Google tells us your name, your email and that the email is yours. We ask for nothing else, and never for your Google password.

Who sees it

  • The people of your projects see your name and picture. Your email and phone are visible to them only if you choose so; the project's admins always see them, to reach you.
  • Your ID number, if you give it, is seen only by you and your projects' admins.
  • Files and everything else in a project are seen only by its people, never from outside.
  • The {app} team can see what is needed to keep the site working and to help when you ask.

We do not sell your data, show ads or share it with anyone else. Emails (invitations, confirmations, notices) are delivered through an email provider, which only carries them.

What is recorded

So that everything stays checkable, {app} records what changes (what was added, changed or removed, by whom and when) and each time someone signs in. It never records what people read. When something goes wrong, it notes where, and who was using it, to fix it. Only the {app} team sees these records.

Cookies and your device

{app} uses only the cookies it needs: one keeps you signed in, one protects your forms, and a few remember your language and appearance. There are no advertising or tracking cookies.

So that the app works without a connection, your device keeps a copy of the pages you visited. It is forgotten when someone else signs in on that device. Your device also remembers which guided tours you have already seen.

If you turn on notifications on a device, its browser's push service (Google's, Mozilla's or Apple's, depending on the browser) carries them to it, encrypted: it cannot read them.

How long

Your data is kept while you have an account. When a project ends or is deleted, its data is kept only as long as its records need it. The record of each lottery is kept, so that its result can always be checked.

Your rights

You can see and correct your data in your profile at any time, and delete your account there. You can also ask us for a copy of it, to correct or update it, or to delete your account, by writing to {contact}. We answer within the terms of Uruguay's personal data protection law (Ley N.º 18.331); you can also turn to its authority, the Unidad Reguladora y de Control de Datos Personales (URCDP).

Changes

If this page changes in anything that matters, we will say so in the app before it applies.
