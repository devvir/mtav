Admin guide

Admins set the project up and run its lottery. They work on the same pages as everyone (there they add units, families and events, draw the plan and run the lottery), plus Settings, at the end of the menu, with three tabs: Setup, Invitations and Project. Quick actions, at the top, gathers the most frequent ones. Each ? explains its page and, where there is a guided tour, offers Tell me more. This guide complements the member guide, which holds for you too.

Starting a project

Anyone with a confirmed account can start a project (Start a project, in the projects menu), and becomes its first admin. A new project is limited, with room for most cooperatives: people, families and units, invitations a day, size and storage of files, a lottery of as many families as the limit, and how fast each person may use it. The {app} team can make it unlimited: ask from Setup, under Limited, whenever you like; you are told when it is. Until the lottery runs, the project's home, Setup and Lottery show the path to the lottery, step by step (1): the unit types, the units, the invited families, the lottery's date and the lottery itself.

Setting it up

  1. Unit types: groups of equivalent units, such as "2 bedrooms", with a size on the plan if you like. Each type has its own lottery.
  2. Units: each with its type. Add and continue with another lets you add many quickly, and you can create a new type on the way; they can also be added from the plan's palette. Units shows them by type (1), and from there you edit or remove types and units: not a type in use, nor a unit a family has already received. Once the lottery has run, a unit's or a family's type no longer changes.
  3. Families: each with its type, and someone invited into it (Add and invite). There should be as many units as families of each type. Requests to join a family are answered by the family, not by admins. A family is removed only once nobody is left in it; a family that empties is archived by itself and you are told: under Show archived you see its history, invite someone into it to bring it back, or delete it for good.
  4. Invitations: invite people into their family, as guests or as admins. Then families can invite their own people.
  5. Plan: place the units and draw what surrounds them, on as many floors as needed.

Drawing the plan

In Units, tap Edit the plan (on a computer: drawing needs a large screen and a mouse). One admin draws at a time. The others are told who is drawing.

  1. Add what you need from the palette (1): units (their details first), streets, green areas, common spaces, parking, playgrounds, entrances and other shapes. Units added elsewhere wait below, under To place.
  2. Drag a shape to move it, its corners to resize it and an edge to move that side; the round handles move a vertex, and a double click on an edge adds one. It aligns with what surrounds it, with a dotted guide. Ctrl + drag makes a copy.
  3. A right click has everything about a shape: naming it (a double click too), rotating it, its color, one floor up or down, bringing it to the front or sending it to the back, duplicating it, copying and pasting it, or removing it. A unit is removed from its details, not from the plan. At the top are Undo and Redo, Edit the boundary of the site, Snap to grid and the floors.
  4. It saves itself (2); when it cannot, it says Not saved yet and tries again. Done editing closes it. After a long while without changes, it asks whether you are still drawing and then closes, saved, so that others can draw.

Who joins, guests and features

In Settings, Project, are its name, its description and its organization, and What it has (1): events, gallery, files, forum, chat and the chat Assistant. A feature turned off is hidden and keeps what it holds; the lottery is always there. The rarer choices are folded at the end, under Advanced settings. Show it in Find a project lets anyone with an account find it (only once the project is unlimited), and then you choose who can join: Those the admins accept or Anyone, at once. Allow guests admits people who have not joined their family yet: they take part in everything but the lottery, invite nobody and see no contact details. Without guests, whoever comes in first joins their family; and guests cannot be turned off while there is one, or an invitation on its way to one.

Invitations

In Invitations, invite each person by email into their family, as a guest if the project allows them, or as an admin (1). An invitation is valid for two weeks; those waiting for an answer stay in view, with their date, and you can withdraw them. A new one to the same address replaces the earlier one. A limited project shows how much room it has left.

Someone already in the project without a family, and not an admin, joins the family at once, and is told. If you show the project in Find a project and choose that admins decide, those asking to join appear here too, for you to accept or decline; someone declined can ask again after a month.

People and roles

In People, under Families, Members or Admins, open a person: Change role or family moves them to another family, leaves them without one (if the project allows guests) or makes them an admin; Remove from the project takes them out, with their pending invitations. Nobody can be moved into a family that declined them. An admin can also be in a family. Admins are peers: none takes the role from another, nor removes one; only a superadmin can, as a last resort. You give your own role up from your card, Give the admin role up: you stay in your family, or as a guest where the project has them, and otherwise you leave the project. A project always keeps at least one admin, so the last one cannot. Admins see everyone's email, phone and ID number.

Events

Create an event: on site, with its place, or online, with its link. Drafts are seen by admins only, until published; publishing tells the whole project. An event can ask whether people will go, and everyone sees who is going. From the event you edit it or cancel it: a cancelled event is archived and can be restored. The lottery has an event of its own, which is not edited like the others.

Forum, chat and files

In the forum, admins can pin a topic to the top (1), close it (2) (it can be read, but only admins can go on replying), rename it and remove it, and remove any post. A removed topic is archived and can be restored. The same holds for files: you can remove any, and restore it.

The chat is not moderated: a conversation is read only by those in it, admins included. When someone tells the Assistant something that is the project's business, it may offer them to write to an admin: an ordinary conversation opens with one of you. What people send the {app} team from the Assistant does not go through the admins.

The lottery

In Lottery, set its Date, from when it can run, and a description everyone reads at the top of the page. Families can change their ranking until the lottery starts. Readiness shows, type by type, whether there are as many units as families. From the date on, Run the lottery (2): at least two families are needed, and you confirm by typing RUN. If the numbers do not match you are asked to confirm: what is left over of each type goes into one last round among everyone left over, so a family may receive a unit of another type, and only if there are more families than units in all does one end up without a unit. Everyone watches the lottery work, and everyone is told. The result is final and recorded step by step: under Runs, Its record shows it, and from there admins download the full record. If a run fails, you are told and it can run again.

Only in exceptional cases (a family registered with the wrong type, a family that was missing…), invalidate the lottery, with a reason: every assignment is cleared, and everyone in the project reads who did it and why. Then it can run again, and the earlier one stays on record.

Its lists

In Settings, Project, Download its lists gives the people, the families and the units as spreadsheets, for the cooperative; the people's with or without email, phone and ID number. Personal data is never used or shared without people's consent.

Deleting the project

In Settings, Project, by typing its name; not while a lottery is running. A project with a history (other people, invitations, events, files, a lottery) is archived for the platform's records: it disappears for everyone, and only a superadmin can bring it back.
