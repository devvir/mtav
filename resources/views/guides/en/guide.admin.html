Admin guide

Admins configure a project and run its lottery. Their tools are in the Administration group of the menu: Setup lists, in order, what is left to set up. Every ? explains its page, and offers a guided tour.

Starting a project

Anyone with an account can start a project, and becomes its first admin. A new project is limited, with room for most cooperatives (people, families and units, invitations a day, file storage); the MTAV team can make it unlimited: ask from Setup, under Limited, whenever you like. Until the lottery has run, the project's home and Setup list what is left to do, in order (1).

Setting it up

  1. Unit types: groups of equivalent units, like "2 bedrooms". Each type is its own lottery.
  2. Units: each with its type. Add and continue loads many quickly; a new type can be created on the way. Units lists them by type (1).
  3. Families: each with its type. There should be as many units as families of each type.
  4. Invitations: invite people into their family (or as fellow admins). Families can then invite their own people.
  5. Plan: place the units and draw what surrounds them, on as many floors as needed.

Drawing the plan

On Units, press Edit the plan (on a computer: drawing takes a large screen and a mouse). One admin draws at a time: anyone else is told who is drawing.

  1. Add what you need from the palette (1): units (their details first), streets, green areas, common spaces, parking, playgrounds, entrances.
  2. Drag a shape to move it, its corners to resize it, an edge to move that side; it lines up with what is around it, with a dashed guide.
  3. Right-click a shape for all it takes: its name, rotating it, its colour, a floor up or down, bringing it to the front, copying or removing it. Double-click it to name it.
  4. It saves by itself (2); Done editing closes it. Left alone too long, it asks whether you are still drawing, then closes, saved, so others can draw.

Who can join, guests and features

In Settings, Project, choose whether the project is listed in the directory, who can join, whether it allows guests (people with no family, who take part in everything but the lottery, and do not see contact details), and which features it has: events, gallery, files, forum and chat. A feature turned off is hidden and keeps what it holds. Without guests, whoever joins creates their family first.

Invitations

In Invitations, invite each person by email into their family, or as a fellow admin (1); the pending ones are listed until accepted.

Someone already in the project with no family enters the family at once, and is told by email. If you list the project in the directory (Project tab), those asking to join appear here too, for you to accept or decline.

People and roles

In People, viewing Everyone, you move someone to another family, make them an admin, or remove them from the project. An admin may also belong to a family. A project always keeps at least one admin.

Events

Drafts are visible to admins only, until published. An event can ask people whether they will go; you see who goes.

The lottery

Set its date in Lottery: families can change their rankings until then. Once the date arrives, check that each type has as many units as families, and run it. If the numbers differ you are asked to confirm, and some families or units are left over. The result is final and recorded step by step.

Only in exceptional cases (a family registered with the wrong type, a missing family…), invalidate the run: every assignment is cleared, and everyone in the project reads who did it and why. The lottery can then run again; the earlier run stays on record.

Deleting the project

In Settings, by typing its name. A project with a history (other people, events, files, a lottery) is archived for the platform's records: it is gone for everyone, and only a superadmin can bring it back.
