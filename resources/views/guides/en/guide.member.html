Member Guide

Welcome to MTAV, the application your cooperative uses to manage its housing assignment. This guide explains everything you can do: register your family's preferences, take part in the life of the project, and check the lottery result.

Getting started

Your account

MTAV is invitation-only: someone from your cooperative (an administrator, or a member of your own family) created your account and you received an email with a link. Open it, choose your password and complete your details. From then on you sign in with your email and password.

Finding your way around

  • Navigation lives in the left sidebar: Dashboard, Lottery, Families, Members, Gallery, Events and Documents. On mobile, tap the menu button to expand it.
  • Your personal settings are behind your name, at the very bottom of that same menu.
  • The + (Quick Actions) button, top right, gathers the creation actions available to your role — for example, inviting a member of your family.

Your family

In MTAV the unit is the family, not the person: your family will receive exactly one home, and preferences belong to the family (any member can edit them; everyone sees the same order). Each family is assigned a unit type (e.g. "two bedrooms"): your family takes part in the lottery for the units of its type.

You can invite other members of your family — only yours — so they get their own account. Each one receives their invitation by email, just like you did.

Preferences: your part in the lottery

This is the most important action. In the Lottery section you will see the units of your family's type. Order them from most to least desired:

  • Drag units to reorder them, or use each unit's move up and move down buttons (they work with the keyboard too).
  • Check the project plan to locate each unit before deciding.
  • You can change the order as many times as you like until the lottery runs; once it does, preferences are locked automatically.
  • If the project changes (a unit is added or removed, or your family changes type), your list adjusts automatically: you don't lose what you already ordered.

Privacy: nobody outside your family sees your preferences — no other families, neither before nor after the lottery.

The lottery and its result

When the administration runs the lottery, the system assigns all units at once, seeking the best outcome for everyone: first it guarantees the least-favored family does as well as possible, then it maximizes overall satisfaction. The result is final: it cannot be edited or negotiated. In the Lottery section you will see which unit each family received.

Life of the project

Events

Events lists the project's meetings and assemblies (on-site or online) and the lottery itself with its date. If an event asks for attendance confirmation, you can answer whether you're going; the confirmation list is visible to the whole project.

Gallery and documents

Anyone in the project can share images, documents and audio: construction photos, meeting minutes, assembly materials. What you upload is associated with your name; you can edit its description or delete it (an administrator can also remove content).

Notifications

The application keeps you posted on what happens in your project — new events, changes, the lottery result — through notifications you can review and mark as read.

Your settings

Tap your name (bottom of the sidebar) → Settings:

  • Profile: your first and last name, legal ID, phone, photo and a short "about me". Also your email address: when you change it, the new address stays pending verification until you confirm from the link you receive — your account keeps working with the previous address meanwhile, so a typo can't lock you out.
  • Password: change it anytime. If you forgot it, use "forgot password" on the sign-in screen.
  • Appearance: light, dark or automatic mode (following your device), and several color themes — including a high-contrast one for better readability.

Questions?

Check the FAQ from this same documentation section, or contact one of your project's administrators.
