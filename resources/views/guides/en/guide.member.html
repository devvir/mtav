Member guide

{app} is where your cooperative organizes the allocation of its homes. Here you follow the project, talk with the other families and rank the units your family prefers. This guide and the questions and answers are always in the menu under your picture, as Guide and Frequently asked questions. In the app, look for the ? icons: they explain each page and, where there is a guided tour, offer Tell me more. The first time you open a page, its tour plays by itself; you can turn tours off whenever you like, in your profile. And to ask something right away there is the Assistant, in your messages.

Your project's home

The first page shows the project at a glance: its families, members and units (each number leads to its page (1)), the lottery once it has a date, what was shared lately, what is coming up, the project's people and the units by type.

Joining

You join a project with the invitation that reaches you by email, from an admin or from someone in your family. It is valid for two weeks. If you have no account, you choose a password (or sign in with Google, where available). If you have one, you sign in and accept: the invitations waiting for you are also listed in Your projects. One account serves all your projects; the menu with the project's name, at the top, takes you from one to another.

You can also look for a project yourself: Find a project, in that same menu, shows those that let themselves be found. Some let you in at once (Join). In others the admins decide (Ask to join): you are told when they answer, and meanwhile you can withdraw the request. A project without guests that has no unit types yet, or whose lottery has run, takes nobody new.

Once in, you join your family. If the project allows no guests, that is the first and only thing you see on arrival: find your family, ask to join it or create it if it is not there. If the project allows guests, you can take part without a family for a while: a guest takes part in everything but the lottery, invites nobody and sees nobody's email or phone; whenever you like, Join your family, in the menu, takes you to find it.

Your family

In the lottery your family takes part as one: it shares one ranking and receives one unit. In People you see the cooperative three ways, Families, Members and Admins (1), with one search for all three. Open anyone to see their family and what they say about themselves; their email and phone, only if they share them.

  1. Open your family's card.
  2. Tap Invite a family member (1): your family is already chosen. The same button is in the menu.
  3. Type their email and send it: the invitation reaches them. If that person is already in the project without a family, they join at once. The invitations waiting for an answer stay in view, and you can withdraw them.

Joining a family, or changing it

Not in your family yet? Find your family shows the project's families. Ask to join yours, and anyone in it can accept you: they are told. One request at a time, and you can cancel it. If it is not there, create it (Mine is not here: create it). If a family declines you, you cannot ask again, unless someone in it lifts the decline or invites you.

In the wrong family? Open your family's card and tap Wrong family?, at the very bottom, to ask to join the right one or create it. You stay where you are until it is settled. On that same page, if the project allows guests, you can simply Leave my family; and from your own card, Leave the project. A family with nobody left in it is archived, its ranking kept: it comes back when an admin invites someone into it, or when someone who was in it creates it again under the same name. Once the lottery has run, families no longer change.

The units

Units shows every unit of the project, by type, and the plan of the site: those of your family's type stand out and, after the lottery, yours. Open a unit to see it on the plan.

Ranking the units

In Lottery you rank the units of your family's type, from the one you want most to the one you want least. Every change is saved as you make it, for the whole family, until the lottery runs. Only your family sees its ranking; the others, never.

  1. Move a unit in the list (1): drag it, use its arrows, or pick it up with the space bar and move it with the keyboard arrows (Escape drops it where it was).
  2. Or use the plan, with a mouse or a pen: drag a unit from the plan onto one of the numbered places below it. The unit you tap on the plan lights up in your list, on the phone too, where you rank from the list.

The plan zooms like a map: hold Ctrl (⌘ on a Mac) and use the wheel, pinch on the phone, or use its buttons. Zoomed in, you drag it to move around. The button in the corner shows it in full screen.

The lottery

From its date on, an admin runs the lottery, and everyone watches it work step by step. While it runs, and once it has, rankings no longer change. The lottery first takes care of the family that would be worst off, and gives it the best it can. Then it looks for the greatest satisfaction of the whole project. Only ties are settled at random. The result is final: you are told, and in Lottery you see which unit each family received. Check the result opens the lottery's record: each family with its unit and the place it held in its ranking, and your family's ranking as it was recorded. A while after the lottery (two days, unless the platform's team sets otherwise), every family's ranking is there too, and the whole record can be downloaded, so that anyone can check the result; the models the lottery is solved with are there for anyone, any time. If an admin ever invalidates a lottery, everyone sees who did it, when and why, and it runs again.

Life in the project

Each project chooses what it has: events, gallery, files, forum, chat and Assistant. What yours does not use is not in the menu.

  • Events: meetings, assemblies and work days, Upcoming and Past. When an event asks, say whether you will go (1), and you see who is going. An online event has its link to join.
  • Media: photos and videos in one tab, documents and recordings in Files, for the project's people only. Share yours (1), several files at once with one description; later you can edit it or remove what you shared. Open a photo and move to the next with the arrows, the keyboard or a swipe. Recordings play right there, faster if you like. If the project has only one of the two, the menu says Gallery or Files.
  • Notifications: the bell tells you what happens in your projects, as it happens, and each notice takes you where it happened. See all notifications opens the full list. In your profile you can have them on your device too, with the app closed. What matters most (invitations, requests to your family, the lottery) also reaches you by email. Chat messages do not go through the bell: they have their own notice.

The forum

Forum is for the whole project's conversations: questions, ideas and news that everyone can read and answer. Open a topic (1) asks for a title and your message. Topics pinned by the admins come first; then those with the latest replies. A search finds topics by their text.

Inside a topic, you reply at the end (1). You can edit or remove your own posts, rename a topic of yours, and remove it while nobody has replied. A topic closed by the admins can be read, but takes no more replies. A new topic is announced to the whole project; a reply, to those who already wrote in it.

Messages

The chat is for talking with one person or with a group. On a computer or a tablet it is always at hand: the round button at the bottom right corner (1) opens the window over the page you are on, and folds it back. On the phone, Messages is in the menu. The number on the button counts the conversations with something new.

  • New conversation (2): pick one person, or several for a group with a name. With each person you have one conversation; groups have their own tab. Every project also has the Community group, with all its people.
  • In a group, the conversation's menu lets you Rename the group, Add people and Leave the group. The pin keeps a conversation at the top of the list.
  • Enter sends; Shift+Enter starts a new line. A message of yours can be edited or deleted for an hour after sending; then it stays as it is.
  • A conversation is read only by those in it: not the admins, not anyone else. For what concerns everyone, there is the forum. New messages reach your device, if you allowed it, not the bell.

The Assistant

The Assistant is an automatic helper for questions about the app. It is the first conversation in your messages, Assistant, in every project that has it; outside any project, in Your projects, the chat is the Assistant alone. The first time you arrive it greets you and offers a tour of the page.

Ask it in your own words how anything works: it explains and, with a button under its answer, takes you there or plays that page's tour. Before your first question it offers a few with one tap, such as What can I do here? or How does the lottery work?. If what you tell it is a matter for your project, it may offer to write to an admin, in an ordinary conversation; if it is a problem or an idea for the app, it may offer to send it to the {app} team, in your words: it is sent only if you tap it. It knows only the app and nothing about other people; it is sometimes wrong, and it has a limit of questions a day. A question of yours can be deleted, and its answer goes with it.

Your account

The menu under your picture holds Profile, this guide and the questions and answers, and Sign out. Your profile has three parts:

  • About you: your picture, your name, your email, your phone, your ID number (which only admins see) and a few lines about you. The admins of your projects always see your email and phone; everyone else, only if you choose so there. If you change your email, your password is needed (if you signed in with Google and have none, you ask for a link to choose one right there) and we send a confirmation to the new address: it changes when you confirm it. At the very bottom you can delete your account.
  • Password: changing it.
  • Preferences: the language (on all your devices, and in your emails); the appearance, on this device only (text size, light or dark, colors, high contrast); the guided tours; and notifications on this device. On a large screen, text size, language and light or dark are also at the top right.
