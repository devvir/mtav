Member guide

MTAV is where your cooperative organizes the assignment of its homes: you follow the project, share with the other families, and rank the units your family would like. Look for the ? icons in the app: they explain each part, and many offer Tell me more, a short guided tour.

Your project's home

The first page shows the project at a glance: its families, people and units (each one leads to its page (1)), what was shared lately, what is coming up, and the lottery.

Joining

You join a project from the invitation you receive by email. If you have no account, you choose a password; if you already have one, you sign in and accept. One account serves for every project you take part in.

You can also find a project yourself: Find a project, in the project menu, lists those open to it. Some let you in at once; in others the admins decide. You enter with no family: create yours, or ask someone in it to add you with your email.

Your family

In the lottery your family takes part as one: its members share one ranking, and it receives one unit. In People you see the cooperative by family or everyone (1).

  1. Open your family's card.
  2. Press Invite a family member (1): your family is already chosen.
  3. Write their email and send: they receive the invitation.

Ranking the units

In Lottery you rank the units of your family's type, from the most to the least wanted. Every change is saved at once, for the whole family, until the lottery runs. Nobody but your family sees your ranking.

  1. Move a unit in the list (1): drag it, use its arrows, or pick it up with Space and move it with the arrow keys.
  2. Or use the plan: drag a unit from the plan onto a numbered place under it. A unit you click on the plan lights up in your list.

The plan zooms like a map: hold Ctrl (⌘ on a Mac) and scroll, pinch on a phone, or use its buttons. Its corner button shows it over the whole window.

The lottery

On its date, an admin runs the lottery, and you see it work step by step. It first makes the family that would be worst off as well off as possible, then the whole project as satisfied as possible; only ties are broken at random. The result is final, and you see which unit each family received. If an admin ever invalidates a run, everyone sees who did it, when and why.

Life of the project

  • Events: meetings, assemblies and work days. When an event asks, say whether you will go (1).
  • Files: photos, videos, documents and audio, for the people of the project only. Share yours (1); open a photo and move to the next with the arrows, the keyboard or a swipe. Recordings sit with the documents, under Files, and play right in their row, faster if you like.
  • Notifications: the bell tells you what happened, as it happens.

Your account

From the menu of your picture: your profile, your password and your preferences: the language (on every device, and in your emails) and the appearance (light, dark, colours, high contrast). Changing your email sends a confirmation to the new address; it changes once you confirm it.
