Member guide

MTAV is where your cooperative organizes the assignment of its dwellings: you follow the project, share with the other families, and rank the units your family would like. Look for the ? icons in the app: they explain each part, and many offer Show me how, a short guided tour.

Joining

You join a project from the invitation you receive by email. If you have no account, you choose a password; if you already have one, you sign in and accept. You can also accept pending invitations from Your projects. One account serves for every project you take part in.

Your family

In the lottery, your family takes part as one: its members share one ranking, and it receives one unit. You can invite the rest of your family from Invite to my family.

Ranking the units

In Lottery you see the units of your family's type, from the most to the least wanted. Move them with the arrows or by dragging them: every change is saved at once, for the whole family, until the lottery runs. Units you do not move keep a place of their own at the end. Nobody but your family sees your ranking.

The lottery

On its date, an admin runs the lottery. It first makes the family that would be worst off as well off as possible, then the whole project as satisfied as possible; only ties are broken at random. The result is final, and you see which unit each family received. If an admin ever invalidates a run, everyone sees who did it, when and why.

Life of the project

  • Events: meetings and assemblies. When an event asks, say whether you will go.
  • Files: photos, videos, documents and audio. Only people in the project can see them.
  • Plan: where each unit is; your family's type is highlighted.
  • Notifications: the bell tells you what happened, as it happens.

Your account

From the menu of your picture: your profile, password, and appearance (light, dark, high contrast). Changing your email sends a confirmation to the new address; it changes once you confirm it.
