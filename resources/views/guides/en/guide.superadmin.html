Superadmin guide

Superadmins look after the platform from the Platform console (in the menu of your picture). You can also enter any project and do anything its admins can; see the admin guide.

Approving projects

Anyone can start a project. Until approved, it works within the limits set for the platform (people, families and units, invitations a day, file storage, lottery size), which keeps abuse in check. Projects lists those asking for approval first: approve a project when you trust its admins. Its limits are lifted and its people are told. Projects you start yourself are approved at once.

Accounts

Accounts lists everyone with an account. The superadmin role is given from a page of its own, Superadmins, by exact email: a rare role, given with care.

Archived projects

When admins delete a project with a history, it is archived: Projects shows it under Archived. Restore it from there, or delete it for good after a summary of what goes with it.

Settings

Settings holds what applies to every project. The lottery solves in an instant, which feels like nothing happened: set how long it takes as people see it (1), step by step. The result is the same.

A new deployment

A deployment with no account at all opens only once its owner, its first superadmin, is set in its settings (OWNER_EMAIL, OWNER_PASSWORD); until then it shows only that it is under construction.

Notifications

You are told when a project is started, and when one asks for approval.
