Superadmin guide

Superadmins look after the platform from the Platform console: you land there when you sign in, and reach it from the menu under your picture. You can also enter any project and do what its admins do: see the admin guide.

A superadmin account is a support account: it is in no project, and reaches every one. It can use each project's chat, forum and files, but only to try them out: what you write there, the project's people read. A bar above every page of a project reminds you. The personal stays out: you do not rank units for a family, you do not answer an event, and you read a chat conversation only if you are in it, like anyone. Admins are peers and none takes the role from another: when one must lose it (an account taken over, say), you do it, from the person’s card in the project, or remove them from it.

Approving projects

Anyone with a confirmed account can start a project (up to two unapproved at a time). Until it is approved, it has the limits set for the platform (people, families and units, invitations a day, size and storage of files, the lottery up to the families limit, and how fast each person may use it), which prevents abuse; its admins only see that it is "limited". Projects shows first those asking for approval, with tabs for those awaiting, the approved and the archived. Approve a project once you trust its admins: its limits are lifted, its people are told, and it can appear in Find a project if they asked for it. Approval cannot be undone. The projects you start yourself (New project) are approved at once, and you name their first admin by email: you take no place in them.

Accounts

Accounts shows everyone with an account, with a search by name or email (1). From each one's menu you can block it (they leave all their projects at once and cannot sign in; their email stays taken, so they cannot join again) or delete it for good (nothing stops them from signing up again, and it is refused while they are a project's only admin). Not your own account, nor another superadmin's, nor the platform owner's. Deleting asks for your password again.

Superadmins

The role is given from Superadmins (1), by inviting an email address that has no account yet, never an existing account: a rare role, given with care, and a support account stays apart from each person's place in a project. The invitation is valid for two weeks, stays in view while it waits and can be withdrawn; it lapses if whoever sent it stops being a superadmin. Remove the role takes it away, except yours and the platform owner's. Inviting and removing ask for your password again.

Archived projects

When admins delete a project with a history, it is archived: Projects shows it under Archived. From there you restore it, as it was for all its people, or delete it for good, files included, after seeing a summary of everything that goes with it, by typing its name and your password. That cannot be undone.

Activity, errors and reports

Activity records every change (what, who, when, in which project) and every sign-in, never what people read. Errors shows each error of the app once, with how often it happened; resolving it makes it disappear, and it comes back if it recurs. Reports gathers what people tell the team through the Assistant, by person: problems and ideas, a few a day each; what needs an answer is answered by email. The platform's owner gets every new error and every report by email.

Settings

Settings gathers what holds for every project. The lottery is solved in an instant, and it looks as if nothing happened. So you can set how long it takes, at least, as people see it (1), step by step: twenty seconds unless you change it, up to five minutes. The result is the same.

The Assistant

The chat Assistant answers with a language model, only when the installation has one configured; without it, it says it is not available and nothing leaves the server. What it knows is these guides and a few facts about whoever asks, never about other people. It has limits per person and per day (half in a limited project or outside any project) and per installation; after failing several times in a row, it rests for a quarter of an hour. The reports it offers to send reach the owner by email and Reports.

Deployments

Deployments appears only for the superadmins authorized in the installation's configuration: deploy any branch to the development environment, deploy the main branch to production, roll back to the release before, or start a test environment over with the demo data. Each action asks for your password again.

A new installation

An installation with no account at all opens only once its owner, its first superadmin, is in its configuration (APP_OWNER: name, email and hashed password). Until then it only shows that it is under construction, except for the guides and the privacy page. The owner always keeps their role and their account, and is the privacy page's contact when no other is set. On test environments, the landing page lets the demo accounts in without a password; in production, never.

Notifications

You are told when a project is created, when one asks for approval, and when someone asks to join a project that was left without admins.
