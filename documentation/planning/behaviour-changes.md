# MTAV: what changed in behaviour, old app vs new app

- **Old app:** commit `2c8e8b13` (`452ca64~1`, 2026-07-13).
- **New app:** the code as it is on `dev` and `staging`. This document follows it: each behaviour change updates the new side.
- **Evidence:** old paths are written `2c8e8b13:path`, new paths plainly.

Every item was read in code, and nothing was run. Claims that could not be confirmed say "unverified" and what was checked. Visual and layout changes are left out.

## Lottery algorithm

Nothing changed in what it does or how.

## Biggest changes

1. **Open sign-up, and anyone can start a project.**
   - Old: no sign-up route at all (`RegisteredUserController` existed but was never routed), and only superadmins created projects (`ProjectPolicy::create` returned `false`).
   - New: anyone signs up (email, which must be confirmed, or Google) and starts projects.
   - A new project is **Limited** until a superadmin approves it on request: 200 people, 50 families, 50 units, 150 invitations a day, 25 MB per file and 500 MB in all, at most 2 unapproved projects per person.
   - A superadmin's own project starts unlimited, and they name its first admin: themselves, or someone invited.
   - Approval cannot be taken back.
2. **Roles are per project.**
   - One account can be in many projects with a different role in each.
   - An admin can also belong to a family, and then ranks units for it.
   - In a project that allows them, someone can also take part as a **guest**: in everything but the lottery.
   - Old: admin or member was global, and a member was in one project only.
3. **Invitations are records that expire and can be revoked.**
   - They last 14 days, and only a hash of the token is stored. Old: the token was the account's first password and never expired.
   - People who already have an account can be invited. Someone already in the project, who has not joined a family here, is put in the family at once, with no invitation to accept; they are told in the app and by email.
   - Invitees accept or decline.
   - Members still invite only into their own family. Guests cannot invite.
   - A blocked account cannot be invited.
4. **Lottery runs are records.**
   - A run is *running*, *completed*, *failed* or *invalidated*, is solved in the background, and is shown as running for a minimum time that superadmins set (20 s by default, 0–300 s).
   - Running is refused while no queue worker has been seen for 120 s. Old: the lottery stayed stuck "executing".
   - A failure notifies the admin who started it, and the lottery can run again.
   - Each run that decided something has a record page for everyone in the project: each family's unit, its place in that family's ranking, the worst and average place, and a SHA-256 fingerprint of what the lottery read and decided. Admins also download the whole trail as JSON, every family's full ranking included.
5. **Invalidation moved from superadmins to project admins.**
   - Only a completed run can be invalidated (409 otherwise). Old: at any time, even mid-run.
   - A reason of 10–1000 characters is required.
   - Every member sees who invalidated, when and why, on the lottery page and in a notification.
6. **Preferences.**
   - Families may rank only some units. Old: they had to rank all.
   - Admins who belong to a family can rank. Guests cannot.
   - Other families' full rankings no longer leak through the lottery page data. Old: the audit records sent to every member held them, with the admin's email.
   - Admins can read every family's ranking in a run's JSON download; everyone else sees only places.
   - The audit is kept as long as the project; deleting a project for good deletes it.
7. **What admins can do to people changed.**
   - Admins can now promote, demote and remove admins (never the last one) and move people between families.
   - Removing someone ends their membership, not their account.
   - Removing a family archives it and removes its members who are not admins from the project; a family with a unit cannot be removed.
   - Admins can no longer edit people's names or emails.
   - Members can no longer edit their own family or its unit type. They can start a new family, and ask to join another.
   - The lottery freezes unit types once it has run.
8. **Deleting a project archives it, or wipes it if fresh.**
   - Old: a project with members could not be deleted at all.
   - A superadmin console (`/admin`) approves projects, restores archived ones and deletes any project for good after its name is typed.
   - It also grants and revokes superadmin (now stored on the account, not an environment list), blocks and deletes accounts, shows the activity and error logs, sets the lottery's minimum time and deploys.
   - Deployers can also start dev or staging over with the demo data; production refuses it.
   - The first superadmin (owner) is created from `OWNER_*` settings.
9. **Files are private to the project.**
   - Old: public URLs anyone could open, and "deleted" files stayed reachable.
   - Removing a file within 60 minutes of sharing it deletes it. Later, it is archived with its file: nobody can open it, and admins can restore it.
   - Types are checked by content. SVG and AVI are refused, and images over 50 megapixels too.
   - At most 20 files per share.
   - Limited projects: 25 MB per file and 500 MB in all, archived files not counted. Unlimited projects go from 100 MB to 200 MB per file.
10. **Notifications.**
    - Old: every change to anything went to the whole project, including draft events' titles, and nothing was ever pushed live.
    - New: chosen ones, pushed in real time on channels that check access, each in its reader's language:
      - lottery completed, failed or invalidated;
      - member joined; invitation received; asking to join a project, accepted, declined; asking to join a family, declined, added to a family, family archived;
      - event published; files, families or units added (the same person's within 30 minutes count as one);
      - forum topic opened, reply in a topic one wrote in;
      - project created, approval requested, approved.
    - Chat messages never go to the bell.
11. **RSVP works.** Old: it existed in the database and in the thesis, but nobody could turn it on or answer. New: admins ask for attendance, each person answers, and attendees are listed.
12. **Privacy, languages, reach.**
    - Email and phone are private unless the person shares them (admins always see them). Guests never see them, shared or not.
    - The ID number (cédula) is seen by the person and by the project's admins and superadmins, and is in the admins' people export.
    - There is a public landing page, a public member manual and a public privacy page.
    - Five languages, chosen per person. Old: one per deployment.
    - Pages can be read offline, and changes are refused while offline.
    - The app installs on a device, under the deployment's name.
13. **A directory of projects, and joining without an invitation.**
    - Old: joining was by invitation only.
    - New: a project its admins list, and a superadmin has approved, appears to every confirmed account. Open: they join at once. Otherwise they ask (at most 10 requests a minute), and the project's admins answer; a declined person may ask again after 30 days.
    - Off by default: not listed, not open, no guests.
14. **Families hold people.**
    - Old: only an admin created a family, and a member's family was set by the invitation.
    - New: someone in a project can start their family, or ask to join one (one open request at a time). Any of its members answers, and nobody else.
    - A decline stands until someone in that family lifts it, against an admin's invitation too.
    - A family left with nobody, no pending invitation and no unit is archived, ranking kept, unless the lottery stands.
15. **Features per project.** Old: everything was always on. New: admins turn forum, chat, gallery, files and events on or off, at creation or in settings. Off, their addresses answer 404 and their content is kept.
16. **An archive inside each project.** Old: nothing inside a project could be restored. New: events, files, forum topics and families that were removed and worth keeping stay hidden for admins, who restore them (at most 200 listed per page). A family comes back only with someone invited into it, or added to it.
17. **Forum.** Old: none. New: anyone taking part opens topics (title up to 160 characters, post up to 20,000) and replies. Admins pin, close and remove.
18. **Chat.** Old: none. New: one on one and groups among anyone taking part, plus one Community group per project. Messages up to 5,000 characters, at most 50 groups per person per project. Only those in a conversation read it: not admins, not superadmins.
19. **The Assistant.** Old: none. New: each person's own conversation in the chat, explaining the app and leading to it.
    - Greetings, thanks and the questions it offers are answered with no model, free.
    - Anything else goes to the model: 40 messages a day per person, 20 when asking from a Limited project, 2,000 a day for the deployment. It rests 15 minutes after 3 failures within 10 minutes.
    - Off unless a key or driver is set, and the signed-in command line is refused in production.
20. **Notifications on devices.** Old: none. New: a per-device switch in the profile, where the deployment has keys. What the bell gets goes to the device, except to whoever caused it, and chat messages too (first 140 characters), except Community and the Assistant.
21. **Accounts can end.**
    - Old: a member or admin could soft-delete their own account, and superadmins could delete any member or admin and restore deleted admins.
    - New: anyone deletes their own account by typing their email, for good, except the owner and a project's only admin. Superadmins can delete any account but a superadmin's and block any but a superadmin's. Blocked, it is signed out, out of every project and its email stays taken; unblocking does not bring the projects back.
22. **Activity and error logs for superadmins.**
    - Old: `/logs` filled only by the seeder.
    - New: every change to 15 kinds of records and every sign-in, failed ones too, with name and IP; kept with no time limit. Errors counted once each, and the owner emailed the first time one appears.
23. **Exports.** Old: none. New: admins download people, families and units as CSV. The people list holds every email, phone and ID number, whatever the person's sharing choice.

## Possibly missing from the new app

Decided with the author. Dropped: admins correcting a person's name or email (it is the
person's own); one invitation making someone admin of several projects; lists spanning several
projects; notifying the admins of several projects of a project's creation or deletion; asking
for phone, ID number and picture on accepting an invitation (they are in the profile; asking
may become a project setting, see the plan).

Still not there, read in code at `routes/web.php` and the controllers named, not decided in this document:

- **Searching or filtering "Your projects".** Old: search and an "active / all" filter. New: the list has neither; the console and the directory have a search. (`app/Http/Controllers/ProjectController.php` index)
- **Restoring a deleted account.** Old: `admins.restore`. New: deleting is final; only blocking can be undone. (`routes/web.php` admin group; `app/Services/AccountService.php`)
- **Pages of their own for unit types.** Old: index and show. New: listed on the units page. (`routes/web.php`)
- **Contacting an admin from the app.** Old: only a placeholder page that said "TODO". New: none, though the chat reaches any admin of one's project. (`routes/web.php`)

Everything else the old app did exists in the new one.

## Accounts & sign-up

### No longer possible / newly restricted

- **Admins editing a person's name or email.** Old: an admin could change any member's first name, last name and email, and the new email took effect at once with no confirmation. A member could also change their own email this way, skipping confirmation. New: admins change only a person's family and admin role. Names and email are changed only by the person, in their profile, and a new email needs confirming. (`2c8e8b13:app/Http/Requests/UpdateMemberRequest.php`, `2c8e8b13:app/Policies/MemberPolicy.php` update; `app/Http/Controllers/Manage/PeopleController.php` update, `app/Http/Controllers/Settings/ProfileController.php`)
- **Everyone's email and phone visible to everyone.** Old: every person's email and phone were sent to every viewer and shown on member and admin cards. New: email and phone are hidden by default. The project's admins, superadmins and the person themselves always see them. Members see them only if the person chose to share them. Guests never see them, shared or not. Searching by email follows the same rule, and guests search by name only. (`2c8e8b13:app/Http/Resources/UserResource.php`, `2c8e8b13:resources/js/components/entities/member/ShowCard.vue`, `2c8e8b13:resources/js/components/entities/admin/ShowCard.vue`; `app/Http/Resources/MemberResource.php`, `app/Models/User.php` scopeSearch, `app/Http/Controllers/Member/PeopleController.php` index, `database/migrations/2026_10_28_000001_add_contact_sharing_to_users.php`, `tests/UseCases/Membership/ProjectIsolationTest.php`, `tests/UseCases/Membership/GuestsTest.php`)
- **Avatar uploads are stricter.** Old: any image up to 2 MB, stored as uploaded. New: JPEG, PNG or WebP only, up to 8 MB and 50 megapixels. The picture is redrawn at 512 px at most as WebP (JPEG where the server cannot write WebP), which strips its metadata, and the previous picture is deleted. (`2c8e8b13:app/Http/Requests/Settings/AvatarUpdateRequest.php`, `2c8e8b13:app/Http/Controllers/Settings/AvatarController.php`; `app/Http/Controllers/Settings/AvatarController.php`, `tests/UseCases/Accounts/AvatarTest.php`)
- **Unconfirmed accounts are kept out.** Old: the `verified` middleware did nothing, because `User` did not implement `MustVerifyEmail`. In practice invitees were marked verified when they accepted. New: an account must confirm its email before it reaches projects, the directory, settings, notifications or project creation. Until then, `/` sends it to the "Confirm your email" page, which offers only resending the link and signing out. It can still open invitation links, the FAQ and the guide. (`2c8e8b13:app/Models/User.php`; `app/Models/User.php` implements MustVerifyEmail, `routes/web.php` verified group, `app/Http/Controllers/HomeController.php`, `app/Policies/ProjectPolicy.php` create, `tests/UseCases/Accounts/WelcomeTest.php`)
- **Confirming an email change needs the right account.** Old: the confirmation link found the user by the id in the URL, so any signed-in session could complete it. New: the link works only when signed in as that account. It also refuses with 409 if the new address was taken in the meantime. (`2c8e8b13:app/Http/Requests/Auth/VerifyPendingEmailRequest.php`; `app/Http/Controllers/Auth/EmailVerificationController.php` verify)
- **Login is also rate-limited per IP.** Old: only the 5-failure lockout per email and IP. New: the same lockout, plus 10 login requests per minute, 10 returns from Google per minute and 5 sign-ups per 10 minutes on the routes. (`2c8e8b13:routes/web/guest.php`; `routes/web.php`, `tests/UseCases/Accounts/SignInTest.php`)

### Newly possible / newly permitted

- **Blocking an account.** A superadmin blocks any account from the console's Accounts page, except their own and other superadmins'. The person leaves every project at once, invitations waiting for their email and their requests to join projects are deleted, and every session ends. The account and its email stay, so they cannot sign in (by password or Google), sign up again with that email, or be invited. Unlike deleting, blocking is not refused for a project's only admin: that project is left with no admin. Unblocking lets them sign in again, but their memberships do not come back. No mail tells them. (`routes/web.php` admin.users.block, `app/Http/Controllers/Platform/UserController.php` block, `app/Services/AccountService.php` block, `app/Http/Middleware/SignOutBlocked.php`, `app/Http/Requests/Auth/LoginRequest.php`, `app/Http/Controllers/Auth/GoogleController.php`, `app/Services/InvitationService.php` invite, `tests/UseCases/Platform/AccountsTest.php`)
- **Open sign-up.** Old: joining was by invitation only. `RegisteredUserController` existed but had no route, and the thesis says "no existe registro público". New: anyone can sign up at `/register` (a dialog over the landing page) with first name, email and password, and optionally last name, then confirms the email. It asks for no consent. (`2c8e8b13:routes/web/guest.php`, `2c8e8b13:app/Http/Controllers/Auth/RegisteredUserController.php`, `documentation/thesis/parte-2-descripcion-funcional.md` §6.2; `routes/web.php`, `app/Http/Controllers/Auth/RegisteredUserController.php`, `resources/js/components/auth/SignUpForm.tsx`)
- **Google sign-in.** Offered only when the deployment sets up a Google client. It signs in the account linked to that Google account, or else the account with that email, or else opens a new account with the email already confirmed. If Google has not verified the email, it never opens or links an account, and a blocked account is refused. If an account with that email was never confirmed, Google sign-in takes it over: it confirms it, sets a new random password and ends that account's other sessions. Someone who only signs in with Google has no known password and must use "forgot password" to get one, because changing the password asks for the current one. (`app/Http/Controllers/Auth/GoogleController.php`, `tests/UseCases/Accounts/GoogleSignInTest.php`, `database/migrations/2026_10_29_000002_add_google_id_to_users.php`, `app/Http/Controllers/Settings/PasswordController.php`)
- **Sharing contact details is a choice.** Two profile switches, "Show my email/phone to the people in my projects", both off by default. (`app/Http/Controllers/Settings/ProfileController.php`, `resources/js/pages/Settings/Profile.tsx`)
- **The old address is told about an email change.** Besides the confirmation link sent to the new address, the current address gets a notice of the change. (`app/Notifications/EmailChangeNotice.php`, `tests/UseCases/Accounts/EmailChangeTest.php`)
- **Resending the confirmation email.** Up to 3 times per 10 minutes. (`routes/web.php` verification.send)
- **A language per account.** The person's preferences hold a language, used for the app and for that person's mail. (See Languages; `app/Http/Controllers/LanguageController.php`, `resources/js/components/account/LanguageSettings.tsx`)
- **Belonging to several projects.** One account can be in any number of projects, with a different role in each. Old: a member belonged to one project, and an account was either an admin or a member everywhere. (`2c8e8b13:app/Models/User.php` is_admin / asMember / asAdmin; `app/Models/User.php` memberships, `documentation/planning/refactor.md` "Per-project roles")

### Works differently

- **Deleting one's own account.** Old: a member could delete their own account, and an admin theirs unless they were a project's only admin (both soft deletes, offered through the card's Delete action when the policy allowed it). New: anyone with a confirmed email deletes their own account in the profile by typing that email; no password is asked. It is a real delete with no restore, and the email may sign up again. It is refused for the deployment's owner and for a project's only admin, and the message names those projects. The person leaves every project, and their memberships, event answers, devices for notifications, Assistant conversations and picture go. A family left with nobody is archived. Their files, events, forum posts, chat messages and lottery records stay, without their name, but the activity log keeps their name as it was, for superadmins. The superadmin role must be removed first, and the owner has no further protection against that. An account that never confirmed its email cannot reach its own delete, and nothing removes it by itself, so this is the only way it goes. - **Someone with no project.** Old: a non-superadmin with no project was signed out and sent back to the login page. New: they land on "Your projects", which shows pending invitations to accept or decline, "Find a project" (the directory) and "Start a project" (if their email is confirmed and they created fewer than 2 unapproved projects). - **Where signing in leads.** Old: to the current project's dashboard, or the projects list for an admin of several. New: superadmins go to the platform console, people in exactly one project go to that project, and everyone else goes to "Your projects". - **Email confirmation covers sign-up too.** Old: the confirmation link was used only for changing email (`new_email`). ## Projects

### No longer possible / newly restricted

- **Deleting a project now archives it or wipes it; it is never blocked.** Before, nobody could delete a project that still had active members (`Gate::denyIf`, which superadmins could not bypass either), and a project without members was soft-deleted and then gone, with no restore route or page. Now an admin (or a superadmin) deletes any project they manage by typing its name. It is archived (soft-deleted, `archived_by` set) if it has a history: a membership of anyone but its creator, any invitation, an event besides the lottery's own, a file, or a lottery run (removed events and files count). Otherwise it is deleted for good, with its files and notifications; units, families, forum topics and chat messages alone do not make it a history. The admin gets the same warning ("cannot be undone") and the same message either way, and nobody is notified. (`2c8e8b13:app/Http/Controllers/Resources/ProjectController.php` destroy; `app/Http/Controllers/Manage/SettingsController.php` destroy; `resources/js/pages/Manage/Settings.tsx`; `app/Services/ProjectService.php` remove/purge; `app/Models/Project.php` hasHistory; `tests/UseCases/Platform/ArchiveTest.php`)
- **An archived project is gone for its people.** Its pages answer 404 for everyone, superadmins too; it appears on nobody's projects page, in the directory or in the switcher; its notifications stop showing; and its pending invitations stop working. Memberships stay, so a restored project comes back with its people. Only superadmins see it, in the console. (`app/Http/Middleware/ResolveProject.php`; `app/Models/User.php` reachableProjects; `app/Models/Invitation.php` scopePending `whereHas('project')`; `app/Models/Notification.php` scopeFor; `tests/UseCases/Platform/ArchiveTest.php`; `tests/UseCases/Membership/DirectoryTest.php` "does not show an archived project")
- **Restoring and deleting for good belong to superadmins.** Before, `ProjectPolicy::restore` let any admin restore, but no route led to it. Now restoring an archived project and deleting a project for good (after typing its name) are console actions, superadmins only. The console page offers both only on archived projects; the delete route also accepts a project that is not archived. (`2c8e8b13:app/Policies/ProjectPolicy.php`; `routes/web.php` `admin.projects.restore` / `admin.projects.destroy`; `app/Http/Controllers/Platform/ProjectController.php`; `resources/js/pages/Platform/Projects.tsx`)
- **Starting projects is capped by how many are unapproved.** A non-superadmin can have at most `limits.unapproved_projects_per_user` (default 2) unapproved projects they created, and needs a verified email to start one. Approved projects do not count, so someone with approved projects still has the cap for new ones. Archived projects do not count, because the query excludes soft-deleted rows. (`app/Policies/ProjectPolicy.php` create; `config/limits.php`; `tests/UseCases/ProjectSetup/ProjectsTest.php` "starts only a few projects…", "needs a verified email…")
- **Unapproved (Limited) projects work within bounds that did not exist before.** The old app had no per-project limits: only a 100 MB per-file cap on uploads (`2c8e8b13:app/Http/Requests/StoreMediaRequest.php`). These limits from `config/limits.php` (`unapproved`; numbers are defaults, each settable by an environment variable) are enforced here:
  - `members` (200): refuses a new invitation once memberships plus pending invitations reach the cap. It is checked only when inviting, not when someone accepts. (`app/Services/InvitationService.php` ensureWithinLimits)
  - `invitations_per_day` (150): refuses invitations once the project has that many created in the last 24 hours; revoked or replaced ones no longer count, because their rows are deleted. It sits on top of route throttles of 60 per minute (admins) and 30 per minute (members). (`app/Services/InvitationService.php`; `routes/web.php`)
  - `families` (50): refuses creating a family at the cap, by an admin or by someone starting theirs, and refuses bringing an archived family back by inviting someone into it; archived families do not count (`app/Http/Requests/FamilyRequest.php` after; `app/Services/InvitationService.php` ensureFamilyTakes). The lottery also refuses to run with more families than the cap, as `too_large` (`app/Services/LotteryService.php` ensureCanRun).
  - `units` (50): refuses creating a unit at the cap (`app/Http/Requests/UnitRequest.php` after). Unit types and plan shapes have no project limit; the plan takes at most 2000 shapes (`app/Http/Requests/PlanRequest.php`).
  - `media_file_mb` (25) and `media_storage_mb` (500): per-file size and total storage (`app/Services/MediaService.php` maxFileKb, ensureStorageAvailable). Approved projects get `media_file_max_mb` (200) per file and no storage cap. PHP's own upload limit caps both.
  - Approval lifts these (`app/Models/Project.php` limit). No route takes approval away again (`routes/web.php`: only `admin.projects.approve`).
- **Limits that apply to every project, approved or not** (`config/limits.php`):
  - `invitation_days` (14): how long an invitation link stays valid (`app/Services/InvitationService.php`).
  - `join_request_retry_days` (30): how long someone declined waits before asking to join the same project again (`app/Models/JoinRequest.php` isSettled).
  - `chat_groups_per_person` (50): how many groups of a project's chat one person may be in, Community aside (`app/Services/ChatService.php` ensureRoomForAGroup).
